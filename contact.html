What happens next

You write or call

Email or call with the city, or with a building address.

We send a sample or a first look

For a sample, we send one redacted listing. For a building check, we tell you how many listings we find before you commit to anything.

You get a quote, if you want more

Pricing is quoted by city and scope. Ask for a free one-listing sample.

You get the report

If you go ahead, you receive a dated report. See an example.

We use public sources only and don't contact hosts or guests. Reports are not legal advice.
