What affects the quote

  • Building size. Prices are per building and depend on the number of units.

Pricing questions

Is there a cost to find out whether anything is listed?

No. Send us the address and we'll tell you how many listings we find before you commit to anything.

What do you need from me for a quote?

The building address (or addresses), the approximate number of units, and whether you'd like a one-time audit, monitoring, or both.

Do you work with owners of a single building?

Yes. Owners with one building are welcome, and there's no account minimum.

Is the report legal advice?

No. Short Stay Data is not a law firm. Reports describe public information as of the dates shown and are not legal advice or legal determinations.
