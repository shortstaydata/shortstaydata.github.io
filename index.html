Why we started

Owners can be held responsible for listings they didn't know about.

We started Short Stay Data to help building owners avoid unfair liability. Under LA's Home-Sharing Ordinance (LAMC 12.22 A.32), the City's guidelines state that a property owner "may be held responsible for Home-Sharing violations occurring on their property," not just the hosts who post the listings (City Planning, Home-Sharing Administrative Guidelines, 2026).

Fines are set per day and adjusted annually for inflation. Under the City's fine schedule effective September 1, 2025, the daily fine is $625.11 for advertising a rental unit as a short-term rental in violation of the ordinance, and $2,500.42 for each day of Home-Sharing beyond the 120-day annual limit without an Extended registration (City Planning fine memo, July 30, 2025). As of October 2026, this is the most recent fine schedule we found published by City Planning.

In 2025, the City Attorney settled a case over a rent-stabilized Hollywood building, alleging that at least 10 units had been used as short-term rentals. The owner and manager agreed to pay $150,000 in civil penalties; they said they had no knowledge of the rentals. Sources: City Attorney announcement, September 2, 2025; Los Angeles Times, September 2, 2025.

The practical answer is to know what's listed in your building, document it, and act quickly.

Our edge

We find the listings other tools miss.

Hosts often pin listings in a neighboring city or a few miles away. We match listings to your building from their photos, descriptions and building details, not just their map pin.

Mislabeled listings

A unit in Koreatown can appear on Airbnb as "Culver City." Searches by city or by radius never surface it. Ours do.

Beyond city lines

City enforcement vendors look only inside their own city's borders, and landlord tools search a fixed radius. We start from your building and work outward.

Facts only

We report what public records show.

Our reports describe listings, addresses and City records. We don't make accusations or legal determinations, and we don't collect or sell hosts' personal details.

A step you can take today, for free

LA property owners can file the City's Property Owner Proactive Prohibition through the Home-Sharing Application and Registration Portal (HARP). Once it's filed, tenants can't register units at the property for Home-Sharing.
