How it works

From a building address to a dated evidence report.

You give us an address. We find what's listed there, check it against City records, and document everything so you or your property manager can act.

Step 1

We search and match listings to your building

We review public listings on major short-term rental platforms in and around your area, including listings pinned in a different city. Each one is matched to your building using its own public text, photos, amenities, layout and reviews.

Step 2

We check City and County records

For each listing we check the City of Los Angeles Home-Sharing registration records. For the building we pull public records: parcel number, unit count, zoning and rent stabilization (RSO) status.

Step 3

You get a dated evidence report

One report for the building, covering every listing and host account we found. Each finding includes the listing link, how we matched it, the registration check result and the date checked. It's ready to hand to your property manager or the City.

Step 4

Optional monitoring

Listings come and go. With monitoring we check daily, alert you the same day a new listing appears, and send a dated monthly record of what we found and what was removed.

What you can do with the report

  • Match units from your own records. Listings rarely show unit numbers. Photos, layout and parking details usually let your team identify the unit.
  • Follow your lease process. Your property manager can use the report when addressing the issue with residents.
  • File the City's prohibition. Owners can file the Property Owner Proactive Prohibition through the City's HARP portal so tenants can't register units for Home-Sharing.
  • Keep a record. A dated report shows when you learned about a listing and what you did next.

We don't offer legal advice, and a report isn't legal protection. It gives you the facts quickly so you can document the issue and act.

Our standards

Verifiable facts, no personal data.

Sourced and dated

Every finding cites a public source and the date we checked it, so anyone can verify it.

No host personal details

We report on listings, addresses and public records. We don't compile or sell hosts' names, contact details or profiles.

No accusations

We say what the records show, such as "no matching registration found." Only the City decides whether a violation occurred.

Public sources only

We don't access private or password-protected data, and we don't contact hosts or guests.

Human-reviewed

A person reviews every match. If we can't confirm a listing is in your building, we say so or leave it out.

Corrections

Anyone, hosts included, can email contact@shortstaydata.com with a correction. If we got something wrong, we fix it.
